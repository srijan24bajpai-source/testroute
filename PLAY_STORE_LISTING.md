**Short description (≤ 80):** Plan whole journeys across trains, flights and buses in India.
**Full description (≤ 4000):**
RoutePilot AI helps you plan an entire journey, not just a single ticket. Tell it where you are going (for example Jammu to Kanyakumari) and it builds complete routes that combine trains, flights and buses, with connection timing checked, and explains the trade-offs.

• Compare fastest, cheapest, best-value and most convenient routes
• See a clear timeline, per-leg costs and the full trip budget for your group
• Filters for budget, arrival deadline, modes, number of changes and overnight travel
• Chat in plain language: "cheapest under ₹8,000", "avoid overnight buses"
• Honest labels: Demo, Estimated, Live, Stale. Prices shown as estimates are never presented as quotes
• Private by design: delete your data and account any time

Important: Some results may be sample (demo) data and are not live availability or bookable fares.
